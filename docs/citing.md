# Citing BayesEoR

BayesEoR has been developed over a series of papers. If you use BayesEoR-JAX,
please cite the papers relevant to your analysis, together with the software
paper.

## Methodology

### Tutt, Sims & de Lera Acedo (2026)

=== "Reference"

    *An optimal latent space for Bayesian foreground uncertainty quantification and
    evidence evaluation in interferometric visibilities.* In preparation.

=== "BibTeX"

    ```bibtex
    @unpublished{Tutt_2026_KL,
        author = {{Tutt}, Jacob L. and {Sims}, Peter H. and {de Lera Acedo}, Eloy},
         title = "{An Optimal Latent Space for Bayesian Foreground Uncertainty Quantification and Evidence Evaluation in Interferometric Visibilities}",
          year = 2026,
          note = {In preparation}
    }
    ```
